# About the Site
404 On The Floor is my personal blog and website—a space where I collect
thoughts, projects, and my personal interests in the realm of
cybersecurity. I'm the only contributor. This is an
[IndieWeb](indieweb.org) site. I publish [blog posts and notes](/blog/)
documenting ideas and learnings. I write about these topics outside my
day job for no purpose other than informing myself and having fun.
Learn more about how I handle your data in my [privacy policy](/privacy).
