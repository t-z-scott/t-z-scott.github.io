# Privacy Policy
tl;dr: no cookies and no tracking.

## Site Information
404onthefloor.com is owned and operated by Taylor Scott from San Jose, California.
I am a private citizen and take the protection of your personal data very seriously,
which is why I minimize my collection of it.

This policy lays out the way your data is handled, the way it is used and shared, 
and what is collected about your visits as a data controller.

## Data Collection and Usage
By visiting this website, your IP address, time and date of access, and limited
information supplied by your browser is available to me progammatically due
to the way the Internet works.

In case of abuse (DDOS, automated attacks, etc.), I reserve the right to log
abusive traffic for analysis and mitigation purposes *only*. When I implement this,
I will update this page with the length of time I am holding the logs. IP addresses
will be anonymized as personally identifiable information (PII).

### Email Communication
I consider initiating communication via email as consent for me to reply. If you do
not want a reply, please mention this in the email sent. Your data is never sold to
others. If you want me to delete our communication, please let me know.

This does not apply to cases where retention of email communication is necessary,
e.g. in legal disputes.

### Encryption
Your connection to this site is secured with HTTPS and TLS. This prevents 
man-in-the-middle attacks where a bad actor eavesdrops on a connection. This website
does not support unencrypted connections.

### Third-Party Privacy Policy Disclaimer

Policy of the external site applies. Also, your browser might send a referrer to
the external site, letting them know where you clicked the link. This is not owned
or able to be changed by me.
