Hello and welcome to 404 On The Floor, the blog where I, Purple Paradox, post about cybersecurity and digital privacy. This is my first post and it's about none of these things. Instead, we're talking about the blog name.

# What's with the name?
Thanks for asking! 404 On The Floor a [portmanteau](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/portmanteau)[^1] of [404](#404-not-found) and [4-on-the-floor](#4-on-the-floor).

## 404 Not Found
A *404 error* is an HTTP response status message that indicates the requested document was not found on this server. This mainly happens when the webpage requested has been deleted or renamed without setting up a redirect, resulting in a dead or broken link (also known as [link rot](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Link_rot)).
## 4-on-the-floor
*4-on-the-floor* is a rhythmic pattern where the bass drum hits on every beat. It's commonly used in pop and dance music, and is, in my opinion, a musical cheat code for danceability. Here's some of my favorite examples of this:
